About Falcon / Since 2008

Made by hand.
Made to last.

We are a small, exacting studio creating mineral surfaces for considered interiors.

The story

Less noise.
More substance.

Falcon Concrete Creation began with a belief that walls should be felt as much as seen. Today our finishers work with architects, designers and homeowners to create rooms with depth and a sense of permanence.

Our materials are honest, our process is collaborative, and the final surface is always made for its particular light.

Our principles

Quietly
obsessive.

Good work is in the details no one can quite point to.

We prepare thoroughly, sample generously and never rush the final pass. That care is what makes a surface feel inevitable when it is finished.
